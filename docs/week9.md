## Week 9 Homework: Landscape Study – Second Pass (Due 10/27)

### Assignment Details
This week, you will continue building upon your outdoor environment from Week 7, refining your existing landscape rather than starting from scratch.  
You will enhance your scene using Landscape Materials, Foliage, and simple player feedback systems such as sound or particle effects.


You may use your own 3D assets or download items from the Fab storefront to supplement your scene. If you use any assets from other sources, include a text file with proper attribution credits.

---

### Criteria

1. Continue from Week 7  
- Use your existing Week 7 Landscape Study as the foundation for this assignment.  
- Continue refining the same level rather than creating a new one.

2. Landscape and Materials  
- Expand and refine your sculpted terrain to improve composition and flow.  
- Create and apply a Landscape Material using multiple textures.  
- Use macro-variation to reduce tiling and add visual interest.  
- (Optional) Convert your material to a Material Instance for easier adjustments.

3. Foliage and Environmental Detail  
- Use the Foliage Tool to populate your scene with props.  
- Create at least two Foliage Types for variation.  
- Experiment with randomization and placement to achieve a natural feel.

4. Player Feedback  
- Add a simple Niagara Particle System to suggest atmosphere or provide feedback for interactions (for example, dust, wind, mist, sparks, or floating particles).  
- Add ambient or spatial sound to create environmental presence (for example, water, birds, or wind).  
- (Optional) Trigger a sound or particle effect from a Blueprint event, such as a player overlap.
